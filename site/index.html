    How to use Weighted Dice

    Profiles

    A profile is always active. Create one per character; class, spec, stats, weapons, slots, mode, weekly-roll math, and bonus-roll history stay on that profile. Use the gear beside the dropdown to rename or delete. Deleting the last profile makes a new Default.

    Mythic+ and Raid

    Switch Mode to plan Mythic+ dungeons or raid bosses. Raid columns are bosses; pick the raid from the dropdown. Include-slots, stat priority, weapons, and bonus-roll wins stay on the same profile in both modes.

    Class, spec, and stats

    Pick your class and spec. Stat priority starts from Wowhead defaults for that spec. Drag the chips or use the arrows if you disagree. Each spec remembers its own order.

    Weapons

    Choose 2-handed or MH/OH. The other style is treated as waste in the remaining pool, so a 2H spec does not count one-hand drops as upgrades.

    Reading the table

    Gold cells with a BIS tag match both of your top two stats. Green-tinted cells are partial matches. Uncheck a slot when you already have that piece from the other mode, crafting, or anywhere else so those drops stop counting.

    Finger and trinket have two checkboxes. One filled slot does not waste the whole row.

    Bonus rolls

    Click the dice on a drop when you win it. The item stays on the table but fades out of the remaining pool. Open Roll History to review wins and remove a mistake; it asks for confirmation.

    The footer math

    Total Items is what is still in that dungeon or boss pool. BIS is remaining items with both top stats. Upgrade has exactly one. Waste is everything else, including filled slots and the wrong weapon style. The percentages are your odds of rolling each from what is left.

    Weekly roll

    Open Weekly roll for ranked scenarios of where to spend a bonus roll. In Mythic+ it ranks dungeons; in Raid it ranks bosses. Both use the remaining pool after your current setup and wins.

    Your session

    Each profile stays in this browser. You can reopen this any time from How This Works.
