    How to use Weighted Dice

    Class, spec, and stats

    Pick your class and spec. Stat priority starts from Wowhead defaults for that spec. Drag the chips or use the arrows if you disagree. Each spec remembers its own order.

    Weapons

    Choose 2-handed or MH/OH. The other style is treated as waste in the remaining pool, so a 2H spec does not count one-hand drops as upgrades.

    Reading the table

    Gold cells with a BEST tag match both of your top two stats. Green-tinted cells are partial matches. Uncheck a slot when you already have that piece from raid, crafting, or anywhere else so those dungeon drops stop counting.

    Finger and trinket have two checkboxes. One filled slot does not waste the whole row.

    Bonus rolls

    Click the dice on a drop when you win it. The item stays on the table but fades out of the remaining pool. Use Remove under the table if that was a mistake; it asks for confirmation.

    The footer math

    Total Items is what is still in that dungeon’s pool. BIS is remaining items with both top stats. Upgrade has exactly one. Waste is everything else, including filled slots and the wrong weapon style. The percentages are your odds of rolling each from what is left.

    Your session

    Class, spec, priorities, weapon style, slot checkboxes, and bonus-roll wins stay in this browser. You can reopen this any time from Instructions.
